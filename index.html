Provider Overview

🟠

Claude (Anthropic)

Best for coding & following instructions

  • Excellent at complex, detailed instructions
  • Industry-leading code generation
  • Natural, human-like conversation
  • Strong reasoning with "extended thinking"
Trade-off: More expensive than DeepSeek/Gemini
Opus 4.5 ($$$) Sonnet 4.5 ($$) Haiku 4.5 ($)
🟢

OpenAI (GPT)

Most widely used, strong all-rounder

  • Largest ecosystem & integrations
  • Best reasoning models (o3 series)
  • Strong multimodal capabilities
  • 1M token context (GPT-4.1)
Trade-off: Premium pricing, especially GPT-5 and o3
GPT-5 ($$$) o3 ($$$) GPT-4o ($$) 4o-mini ($)
🔵

Google (Gemini)

Long context & Google integration

  • Longest context (up to 2M tokens)
  • Native Google Workspace integration
  • Good price-performance ratio
  • Strong multimodal (text, image, video)
Trade-off: Less consistent on complex reasoning
Gemini 3 Pro ($$$) Gemini Flash ($) 2.5 Pro ($$)
🟣

DeepSeek

Incredible value - 10-30x cheaper

  • Quality comparable to GPT-4/Sonnet
  • Fraction of the cost of competitors
  • Open source (can self-host)
  • Strong reasoning model (R1)
Trade-off: Chinese company (compliance considerations)
V3.2 ($) R1 ($) Coder ($)
⚪

Open Source

Self-host for privacy & control

  • Full data privacy - your infrastructure
  • No per-token API costs
  • Customizable & fine-tunable
  • No vendor lock-in
Trade-off: Requires GPUs & technical expertise
Llama 4 Qwen 3 Mistral
